ABOUT IFEOLUWA

Ifeoluwa Opawoye turned his own journey from Nigeria to Australia into a mission. As the founder of AFROVO and a Qualified Education Agent Counsellor (QEAC #T268), he draws on firsthand experience to guide Africans in pursuing study, work, and migration opportunities — with transparency, cultural insight, and a genuine commitment to empowering every client.
